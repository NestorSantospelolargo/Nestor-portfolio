# Exams

## Midterm Examination Reflection

I earned a score of 98 on the midterm examination.

The midterm evaluated my understanding of intelligent agents, search algorithms, Large Language Models, hybrid systems, reasoning methods, and analytical problem-solving.

One of the most important lessons from the midterm involved understanding the limitations of LLMs. Although LLMs can generate fluent and convincing responses, they do not always verify facts or follow logical constraints. The exam highlighted situations where an LLM might provide an incorrect answer despite sounding confident.

The analytical problems reinforced the importance of classical AI methods such as search, planning, logical reasoning, and constraint-based approaches. These methods provide verification and correctness that language models alone cannot guarantee.

---

## Final Examination Reflection

I earned a score of 98 on the final examination.

The final assessment connected many of the major topics covered throughout the semester, including search, intelligent agents, adversarial reasoning, constraint satisfaction problems, ethics, LLMs, and hybrid AI systems.

One of the most important conclusions I reached was that intelligence in artificial systems is not simply the ability to generate convincing language. Effective AI systems must also reason, search, validate information, and make decisions under constraints.

The final reinforced my understanding that hybrid systems, which combine LLMs with structured reasoning and validation methods, will be the most reliable AI architectures moving forward.
