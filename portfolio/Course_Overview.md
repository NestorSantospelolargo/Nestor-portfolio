# Course Overview

Artificial Intelligence has become one of the most influential fields in modern computing and engineering. The purpose of this portfolio is to document my learning experiences, projects, assessments, and reflections throughout ICOM5015 Artificial Intelligence.

This course introduced me to both classical and modern approaches to artificial intelligence, including intelligent agents, search algorithms, constraint satisfaction problems, adversarial search, reasoning systems, Large Language Models (LLMs), and ethical issues related to AI.

Initially, my perception of AI was heavily influenced by modern tools such as ChatGPT and other LLMs. However, the course demonstrated that AI is a much broader field that includes search, planning, reasoning, optimization, knowledge representation, and decision-making systems. By the end of the semester, I developed a more complete understanding of artificial intelligence and its practical applications.

# Connection Between Course Outcomes and My Learning Goals

At the beginning of the semester, my primary goal was to develop a deeper understanding of artificial intelligence beyond my previous experience with tools such as ChatGPT and other Large Language Models. I wanted to learn how AI systems make decisions, solve problems, and can be applied to real-world situations.

## Outcome 1: Describe, Analyze, and Apply Different AI Techniques

This outcome supported my goal of understanding how AI systems actually work. Before taking this course, I mainly associated AI with chatbots and language models. Learning about intelligent agents, search algorithms, knowledge representation, inference, logic, and learning allowed me to see the broader field of AI and understand the different techniques used to solve complex problems.

## Outcome 2: Design an Application of Artificial Intelligence

One of my goals was to gain practical experience with AI rather than only learning theory. The programming assignments helped me apply AI concepts directly and understand how AI systems are implemented. Designing and analyzing AI applications allowed me to connect classroom concepts with real computational problems.

## Outcome 3: Review and Discuss Current AI Literature

I wanted to better understand how AI is being used in the real world and how current technologies relate to the concepts taught in class. Reviewing technical reports, researching AI systems, and discussing topics such as LLMs, hybrid systems, and ethical concerns helped me connect theoretical knowledge with current developments in artificial intelligence.

## Outcome 4: Write and Present a Demonstration of a Technical Paper About AI

Another personal goal was improving my ability to communicate technical information. Writing reports and participating in project presentations strengthened my technical writing skills and helped me explain AI concepts more clearly and professionally.
