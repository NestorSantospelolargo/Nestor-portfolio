# Teamwork and Collaboration

Many of the projects completed throughout this course required teamwork, communication, and coordination among group members. Working in teams provided valuable experience not only with artificial intelligence concepts but also with project management and collaboration.

During the semester, our group encountered challenges related to workload distribution and meeting project deadlines. One team member consistently struggled to complete assigned tasks on time despite reminders from the rest of the group. On one occasion, a significant portion of the assigned work was not completed, resulting in the remaining team members having to wait several hours and ultimately complete additional work themselves to ensure the project could be submitted on time.

As a result of these challenges, our group decided to reorganize and continue future projects with a smaller and more reliable team structure. While this was not an easy decision, it highlighted the importance of accountability, communication, and dependability in collaborative technical projects.

Despite these difficulties, the experience taught me valuable lessons about teamwork. I learned the importance of setting clear expectations, monitoring progress throughout a project rather than only near deadlines, and maintaining open communication among team members. I also gained a greater appreciation for the role that responsibility and time management play in the success of technical and engineering projects.

After restructuring the team, collaboration became more efficient and predictable. Team members were able to divide responsibilities more effectively, communicate progress regularly, and complete assignments in a timely manner. These experiences helped me develop stronger collaboration skills and provided insight into the challenges that often arise in real-world project environments.
