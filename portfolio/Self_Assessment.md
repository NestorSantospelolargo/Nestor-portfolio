# Self-Assessment

Throughout ICOM5015, I developed a significantly broader understanding of artificial intelligence and the many approaches used to solve AI problems.

At the beginning of the semester, most of my exposure to AI came from modern tools such as ChatGPT and other Large Language Models. By the end of the course, I had learned that artificial intelligence is more than language generation and includes search algorithms, intelligent agents, constraint satisfaction problems, adversarial search, knowledge representation, reasoning systems, and hybrid architectures.

One of my greatest strengths throughout the course was my ability to understand conceptual topics related to intelligent agents, reasoning systems, Large Language Models, constraint satisfaction problems, and AI ethics. These topics helped me appreciate the broader field of artificial intelligence and how different AI techniques can work together.

The programming assignments helped me gain practical experience applying AI concepts to solve problems. Through these projects, I learned how theoretical concepts are translated into working systems and how different AI methods can be evaluated and compared.

My performance throughout the course was not completely consistent. I missed several quizzes and did not complete the essays and forum activities assigned during the semester. These missing assignments significantly affected my overall course grade. However, I do believe I have a strong foundation in the course material.

Overall, I believe I successfully achieved the major learning outcomes of the course. Also, I developed a deeper understanding of artificial intelligence and gained a more realistic perspective on both the capabilities and limitations of modern AI systems.

## Areas for Growth

While I developed a strong understanding of many foundational AI concepts throughout the course, there are several areas where I would like to continue growing.

One area I would like to explore further is reasoning in artificial intelligence, particularly reasoning models and systems that can perform structured problem solving. Throughout the course, I learned that Large Language Models are very effective at generating natural language responses, but they do not always reason reliably or verify their conclusions. This, of course, makes me wonder how AI systems can perform more rigorous reasoning, planning, and decision-making.

I would like to learn more about reasoning models, symbolic AI, logical inference, planning systems, and modern hybrid architectures that combine language models with structured reasoning and validation, and how to make them. Understanding how these systems represent knowledge, evaluate alternatives, and arrive at conclusions would help me develop a deeper understanding of artificial intelligence.

In addition, I would like to strengthen my understanding of optimization techniques and advanced search algorithms. Topics such as local search, genetic algorithms, and other optimization methods demonstrated the complexity of decision-making in AI and represent areas where I would benefit from additional study and practice.

As artificial intelligence continues to evolve, I hope to continue learning about explainable AI, trustworthy AI systems, and reasoning-based architectures that improve reliability and transparency. I believe these topics are increasingly important as AI systems are applied to real-world problems.
