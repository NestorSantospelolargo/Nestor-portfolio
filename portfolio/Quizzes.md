# Quizzes

Throughout the semester, quizzes served as tool for reinforcing course concepts and identifying areas requiring additional study.

My strongest quiz performances occurred in topics such as:

- Intelligent Agents
- Large Language Models
- Prompt Engineering
- Heuristic Functions
- Constraint Satisfaction Problems
- Consistency in CSPs
- Syntax and Semantics
- First-Order Logic

Other topics covered throughout the quizzes included search algorithms, local search methods, simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, knowledge representation, and reasoning systems.

My overall quiz performance does not fully reflect my understanding of the course material because I missed several quizzes during the semester. As a result, some quiz grades lowered my overall average despite self studying of the material and doing well on the tests.

Although my quiz performance varied, the quizzes provided valuable feedback and helped reinforce important concepts throughout the semester. They highlighted areas where additional review was necessary and encouraged continuous engagement with the course material.

The quizzes played the important role of keeping me enganged for the midterm and final exams. The quiz topics helped build the foundation necessary to understand more advanced concepts such as Large Language Models, hybrid AI systems, adversarial search, and constraint satisfaction problems.
